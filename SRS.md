1. Introduction:
   1.1 Purpose: The objecitve of this tool is to determine transposable element (TE) invovlvement in enhancers of interest at single-cell resolution
   1.2 Product Scope: This tool is designed to quantify TE activity in open chromatins using the single-nucleus assay for transposase-accessible chromatin with sequencing (ATAC-Seq).
   Although it can also be used for bulk-seq data, the tool development is primary with cell type specific TE-enhancer relationships in mind.
   1.3 References: Interested readers can check out the following publications
      i) He, J., et al. Identifying transposable element expression dynamics and heterogeneity during development at the single-cell level with a processing pipeline scTE. 
        Nat Commun 2021;12(1):1456. doi: 10.1038/s41467-021-21808-x.
      ii) Zu, S., et al. Single-cell analysis of chromatin accessibility in the adult mouse brain. Nature 2023; 624(7991):378-389. doi: 10.1038/s41586-023-06824-9
